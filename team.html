Who we are

EA KC is a Kansas City community for everyone interested in Effective Altruism. We meet around the metro to learn what actually helps, dig into EA-related cause areas, and enjoy each others' company (the conversation still drifts philosophical some nights). We also maintain an active Discord group and have several smaller optional events, such as our book club.
